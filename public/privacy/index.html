Privacy Policy

Planar server at this address · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Planar is an open-source design editor (source code). This policy explains what this Planar server stores, why, who can see it and how to remove it. It covers the web editor, the HTTP API and the MCP endpoint (/mcp) that AI assistants such as Claude use. The server is run by an individual operator, not by a company, and is not connected to Figma or Anthropic.

What we store

DataWhy
Account: username, display name, avatar colour, password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash), sign-up date, last time you were activeTo sign you in, show your name to collaborators and keep your files
Your files: designs, file names, sharing settings (people you invited and their roles, link access), edit timesThis is the service: storing your documents and sharing them as you choose
Version history: earlier snapshots of your files (taken automatically while you or an AI agent edit, and when you save a version)To let you go back; automatic snapshots are deleted after 30 days, named ones when the file is deleted
Comments: text, author, time and position of comments left on your filesTo discuss designs; deleted with the file
Figma imports: the converted design, stored like any other file. Your Figma token stays in your browser: it is sent only to api.figma.com and is not savedTo bring your existing designs into Planar
Team libraries: a copy of the components, variables and styles of a file you publish as a library, and who published itSo people you work with can use them; removed when you unpublish or delete the file (files that already use it keep their copy)
Projects: project names and who you invited to themTo organise files and share many at once
Sign-in sessions: a session cookie (planar_session, HttpOnly); only a hash of it is storedTo keep you signed in for up to 30 days
API keys and connected apps: key names, hashes of keys and OAuth tokens, which app was connected and when it was last usedTo let AI agents you authorised act as you, and to let you revoke them
Usage statistics: daily counts (sign-ups, active users, edits, requests, errors) and per-minute counters for the last 24 hoursTo keep the server running and spot problems; these are counts, not per-person histories
Technical data: IP addresses, kept only in memory for a few minutesTo limit password guessing; IP addresses are not written to disk

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, we do not sell or rent data, and we do not use your files to train AI models. We do not ask for your email address.

What the AI integration (MCP) can access

When you connect an assistant such as Claude, you sign in to Planar and approve the connection on a consent page. The assistant then acts as you, with exactly your rights: it can list and open your files and files shared with you, create files, and edit files you are allowed to edit. Its edits are visible to everyone in the file and can be undone. Whatever the assistant reads through Planar (layer names, text, structure, rendered images) is sent to that assistant’s provider and is handled under that provider’s privacy policy (for Claude: Anthropic’s privacy policy). You can disconnect an app at any time in Planar under your account → Connected apps, or revoke an API key under API keys. Access tokens expire after one hour; unused connections expire after 90 days.

Who can see your data

  • People you share a file with see that file, your display name and username, and — while you have it open — your pointer and selection.
  • Anyone with a link to a file with link access turned on can open it, without an account.
  • Other users can find your username and display name when inviting people.
  • The server operator can see account details, file names, sizes and sharing settings in an admin panel, and can block or delete accounts and files to stop abuse. The admin panel does not open the contents of your files; with direct access to the server the operator technically could, and will do so only when required by law or to keep the service working.
  • Notifications to the operator: the server sends the operator Telegram messages about its health and about new sign-ups; these contain the new username and counts, nothing from your files.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts: when a design uses a web font, your browser loads it from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com, which receive your IP address (Google’s privacy policy).
  • Telegram: used only for the operator notifications described above.
  • Hosting: the data lives on a rented virtual server; the hosting provider operates the hardware but does not process the data for its own purposes.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords, sessions, API keys and OAuth tokens are stored only as hashes. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Files stay until you delete them. Moving a file to the trash hides it from others; Delete forever removes it from the server.
  • Sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out; changing your password signs out your other sessions and connected apps.
  • To delete your account and all your files, or to get a copy of your data, open an issue at github.com/wswfws/Planar/issues (do not post private details there; ask to be contacted privately). You can also download any file yourself as .planar.json.

Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
